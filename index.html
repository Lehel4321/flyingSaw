Model: every move is a trapezoidal point-to-point profile (same acceleration for accel and decel). Max linear speed = motor rpm ÷ gear ratio × travel per output revolution. The saw axis makes three moves while synchronized: rapid approach, cut at cutting feed, rapid retract over the full stroke. The carriage only decelerates once the blade is fully up. Jerk limits, PLC scan time and servo following error are not included — leave some reserve.
