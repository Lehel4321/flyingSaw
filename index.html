    How the model works

    Every move is a time-optimal rest-to-rest profile. With the jerk limit at 0 it is the classic trapezoid (same acceleration up and down). With a jerk limit the acceleration ramps up and down linearly (7-segment S-curve), which makes every move a little longer.

    • Max linear speed = motor rpm ÷ gear ratio × travel per output revolution ÷ 60.
    • The carriage starts when the mark is one ramp distance behind it, so mark and blade line up exactly when line speed is reached.
    • While synchronized the saw axis makes three moves: rapid approach, cut at cutting feed, rapid retract over the full stroke. The carriage only decelerates once the blade is fully up.
    • Motor torque = moving mass × acceleration × lead ÷ (2π × gear ratio), divided by the drive efficiency while motoring and multiplied by it while braking, plus motor and gearbox inertia × angular acceleration. RMS is taken over the full part time, including waiting.
    • Max line speed and shortest part are searched so that every check (speed, cycle time, travel and, when entered, motor torque) still passes.

    Not included: friction and cutting forces, saw axis torque, PLC scan time, cam-table blending between moves, and servo following error. Leave some reserve.
